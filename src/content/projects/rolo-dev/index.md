## Overview

Rolo is a pixel-art companion who lives on your desktop. He roams your screen, eats files dropped on him, chats with you, and naps. He runs locally on your machine, has his own personality, and a memory system.

Explore the interactive project website above and see the [System Design](https://larawashington.github.io/rolo.dev/system-design) page for the full architecture design breakdown.
